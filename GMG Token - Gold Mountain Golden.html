Business Background

Pioneering the Future of Digital Investment

In the evolving landscape of blockchain finance, Real World Assets (RWA) are emerging as a critical bridge between traditional and digital economies. GMG leverages this by tokenizing gold, a timeless safe-haven asset, to create a stable and accessible investment vehicle for a global audience.

The Rise of RWA

As the virtual asset market matures, investors seek stability. Gold, as a premier Real World Asset, offers proven security against market volatility, making its tokenization a logical evolution for blockchain-based investment.

Democratizing Gold Investment

Historically, high entry barriers have limited access to gold investment. GMG removes these obstacles, allowing anyone to own a fraction of physical gold through a secure, divisible, and highly liquid digital token.

Global Asset Accessibility

GMG creates a borderless financial instrument. Users worldwide can access, trade, and utilize the value of gold without the complexities of physical storage, cross-border transfers, or traditional financial intermediaries.

A Dual Token Structure for Trust and Growth

Our ecosystem separates 'store of value' and 'ecosystem activation' to maximize stability and investor returns.

G777 (STO Token)

The Steadfast Anchor of Value

A security token 1:1 pegged to the value of physical gold. G777 is a digital certificate of legal ownership, providing a stable store of value unshaken by market volatility and serving as a premier inflation hedge.

GMG (Utility Token)

The Engine Driving Growth

The engine that promotes all economic activity within the ecosystem. GMG is used for service fees, staking rewards, and governance, with its value directly linked to the vibrancy of the platform.

Gold-Backed Stability

Audited & Verifiable Reserves

GMG’s value is directly tied to tangible, real-world assets. Our commitment to maintaining fully-backed and transparent reserves is the cornerstone of our platform's integrity and your security.

1:1 Backing with Pure Gold

Every GMG token is meticulously backed by one troy ounce of 99.99% pure physical gold, ensuring its intrinsic value is always preserved.

Secure & Regulated Storage

All physical gold is held in high-security vaults at regulated financial institutions in South Korea and the United Arab Emirates, protecting assets under stringent international standards.

Real-Time Price Linkage

The value of GMG is dynamically linked to the real-time international gold price, providing a transparent and fair market valuation at all times.

Independent Monthly Audits

A certified third-party auditing firm conducts monthly reviews of our gold reserves and token issuance, with all reports made publicly available to ensure complete transparency.

Operational Policy

Framework

Our operational policy is designed for stability, growth, and transparency, ensuring the long-term health of the GMG ecosystem.

75% Payment Reserve

Held in physical gold to guarantee the 1:1 backing of all issued GMG tokens.

30% Investment Operations

Allocated for strategic investments to expand the ecosystem and enhance token value.

Smart Contract Automation

GMG's token issuance, transfer, and burning processes are governed by secure smart contracts, which automatically execute based on predefined rules, minimizing human error and enhancing trust.

Transparency and Disclosure

We are committed to radical transparency. All audit reports, reserve balances, and major operational decisions are disclosed to the public through our official channels to maintain community trust and accountability.

Core Asset Partnership

To secure the physical gold backing our G777 tokens, we have established a strategic partnership with Daehan Mining Development Co., Ltd., owner of the Geumpung Mine in South Korea.

Daehan Mining Development: Geumpung Mine

Estimated Gold Reserves: 27,000 tons (approx. 864,000,000 oz)
